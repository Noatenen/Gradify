Sidebar Concept B · not production code

Progress-first

The journey is the hero. A gradient card with a live ring, stage timeline dots and the next deliverable dominates the top of the sidebar — everything a student needs to feel "where am I, what's next" at a glance. Navigation shrinks to a quiet utility list underneath, because it already did its job once you're inside a page.
